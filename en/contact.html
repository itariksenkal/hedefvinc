Good to know

What are your opening hours?
We have no fixed office hours; our phones are open 24 hours a day, every day. Please call before coming to the workshop, as the team may be out on a job.
How do I find the workshop?
We are on the Milas–Bodrum road, at the Tuzla junction between Güllük and Güvercinlik, behind the Shell station. Address: Dörttepe Mah. 3. Bulvar No:128/2, 48200 Milas / Muğla. Use the directions link on this page.
Do you have an email address?
Yes: info@hedefvinc.com. For urgent jobs, phone or WhatsApp is quicker.
Can I send photos instead of explaining on the phone?
Yes, and it often works better. Send photos, a short video and your location on WhatsApp, then call the English line if you want to talk it through.
