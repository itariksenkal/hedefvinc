Your Container Partner in Milas and Bodrum

From our base in Milas, Hedef Vinç supplies new and used containers — for sale or for rent — across Bodrum, Muğla, Aydın and İzmir. Whether you need a site office, a storage unit, a guard cabin or living quarters, we help you choose the container that fits the job. With units available from stock, we can meet most requests at short notice.

What sets us apart is simple: we also operate the cranes. Your container is brought to your address on our own crane trucks, lifted into position and handed over in place. There is no need to hire a separate haulage or crane company — sale, transport and installation are handled end to end by a single point of contact.

What We Offer

  • New container sales — for long-term and corporate use
  • Used (second-hand) container sales — economical and ready to use
  • Container rental — use it for the duration of your project, return it when the job is done
  • Delivery by crane truck — Milas, Bodrum, Muğla and the surrounding area
  • On-site placement — safe lifting and positioning at the exact spot you need

New or Used?

For long-term or corporate use, a new container is usually the better investment; for short-term sites, storage or a temporary office, an economical used container often makes more sense. Every second-hand unit is checked before sale and handed over ready to use. For a temporary need, renting instead of buying is also an option. We are happy to talk through which route suits you best over the phone — just call us for available sizes and fit-out options.

Delivery and Placement

When buying a container, the stage that causes the most trouble is usually unloading and positioning. With Hedef Vinç that problem disappears: because the crane fleet is our own, we bring your container to your address, lower it safely onto the exact spot you choose and set it in place — no third-party delivery company required. From our base between Güllük and Güvercinlik we reach Milas, Bodrum and the wider Muğla area quickly.
