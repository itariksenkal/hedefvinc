Precision Lathe Work in Milas

It is rarely a major breakdown that stops a machine — more often it is one small part. When the shaft or bushing you need is not on the market, when importing it would take weeks, or when your machine is an older model whose spares are no longer produced, the answer is the lathe. At the Hedef Vinç workshop we machine the part you need one-to-one, working from a sample or from measurements.

Machining is a natural extension of our crane and heavy equipment service, so lathe work is part of our daily routine — we know from the field which part has to carry which load and to what tolerance it must run. Our workshop sits between Güllük and Güvercinlik at the Tuzla junction, easy to reach from Milas, Bodrum and the surrounding area.

Parts We Machine

  • Shafts and spindles — plain, stepped and keyed
  • Bushings and sleeves — in steel, bronze and similar materials
  • Pins, bolts and connecting elements
  • Gears and chain sprockets
  • Flanges, couplings and adapter parts
  • Boom, bucket and attachment pins and bushings for cranes and heavy equipment
  • Thread cutting, reaming, grooving and surface machining
  • Re-machining worn parts back to size

One-Off Parts from a Sample or Drawing

Just bring the old, broken or worn part to our workshop. We take its measurements, assess the material and the conditions it works under, and machine an exact replacement. No sample? No problem — we work from your technical drawing, or we come and take measurements on site. The goal is always the same: a part that fits your machine like the original and runs trouble-free for a long time. Before we start, we agree the lead time and the price, so there are no surprise costs.

Who We Work With

We machine parts for crane and heavy equipment owners first of all, along with industrial plants, agricultural machinery users, and transport and construction companies. The marine sector is very welcome too — we produce shafts, bushings, pins and fittings for yachts, boats and boatyards around Bodrum and Güllük. From a single urgent one-off part to small production runs, we serve businesses across Milas, Bodrum, Muğla, Aydın and İzmir.
