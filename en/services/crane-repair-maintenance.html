Crane repair, overhaul and maintenance

If your crane has broken down or is due for an overhaul, we repair it in our own workshop at the Tuzla junction in Milas. If the fault can be fixed on site, we come to you; stripping, welding and full overhauls are done in the workshop. We have been doing this since 2005 and our phones are open 24/7.

What we repair

Hydraulic, mechanical and welding work under one roof, so most repairs do not wait for an outside workshop.

  • Fault finding: pressure and leak checks, control and electrical faults, the source of noises and play.
  • Hydraulics: pumps, control valve blocks, cylinder seals, load-holding valves.
  • Hoses and fittings: burst hoses cut to size and pressed in the workshop.
  • Boom and column: cracks, bending, worn pins and bushes; welding repairs and new pins and bushes from our lathe.
  • Slewing: play, knocking, gears and mounting bolts.
  • Outriggers: outrigger cylinders, lock valves, outrigger beams.
  • Overhauls: strip-down, inspection, renewal, reassembly, paint and test.
  • Servicing: oil, filters, greasing and general checks; we also fix items flagged in periodic inspection reports.
  • Truck-mounted (loader) cranes and marine knuckle-boom cranes on boats and quays.
  • On-site visits across Milas, Bodrum, Muğla and the surrounding area.

Describing the fault

These answers help us bring the right parts the first time.

  • Make, model and year of the crane; is it on a truck or on a boat?
  • When does the fault appear: under load, empty, when cold?
  • Which movement: boom, telescope, slewing, outriggers, winch?
  • If there is a leak, where is the oil coming from? A photo is enough.
  • Where is the crane now, and can it be driven?
SymptomFirst place we look
Boom creeps down under loadCylinder seals and load-holding valve
Movements slow, pump louderOil level, filter, suction line, pump wear
Oil around a hose or fittingLoose fitting, worn hose
Outriggers retract under loadOutrigger cylinder and lock valve
Play or knocking when slewingSlewing bearing, gears, mounting bolts
Control lever sticky or slowControl valve block and linkages

These are possible causes; a firm diagnosis needs us to see the crane. If the boom is creeping down, do not lift with it until the cause is found. Never feel for a hydraulic leak with your hand: a pinhole jet of oil can penetrate the skin.

How a repair works

  1. Tell us about the fault

    By phone or WhatsApp. Photos, a short video and the crane model speed things up.

  2. Diagnosis

    On site or in the workshop. We tell you what can be repaired and what needs replacing.

  3. Approval and repair

    We agree the work and the time with you. Hose pressing, lathe and welding are in-house.

  4. Test and hand-over

    We run every movement and check pressures and leaks. Delivery by truck if needed.

Frequently asked questions

Do I have to bring the crane to your workshop?
It depends on the fault. Many hose, fitting, control and outrigger problems can be fixed where the crane stands. Seal replacement, welding and full overhauls are done in our workshop at the Tuzla junction in Milas.
Can you repair the crane on my boat?
Yes, we work on knuckle-boom cranes mounted on boats and quays. Send us photos or a short video of the fault and tell us where the boat is, and we will plan the visit or the removal with you.
How long does an overhaul take?
It depends on the condition of the crane and the parts it needs. Once we have stripped and inspected it, we tell you what has to be done and how long it will take, including any parts that must be ordered.
What if a part is no longer available?
Pins, bushes and shafts can be made from a sample or from measurements on our own lathe. Hydraulic hoses are pressed in the workshop, usually the same day. For pumps and valves we discuss the options with you.
How do you price a repair?
By the scope of the fault, the parts needed and whether the work is done on site or in the workshop. We look at the crane first, tell you what needs doing and the price, and only start with your approval.
