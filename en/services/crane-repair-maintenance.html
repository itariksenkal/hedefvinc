Crane Repair & Maintenance

Fault diagnosis, overhaul, repair and periodic maintenance for cranes of every brand. We get your crane back to work as quickly as possible — in our Milas workshop or on site at your location.

Why Is Regular Crane Maintenance So Critical?

A crane works under heavy loads, and a small oversight can quickly turn into a serious risk. Maintenance that is put off leads to hydraulic leaks, worn wire ropes, control faults and unplanned downtime — threatening both site safety and the continuity of your work. At our facility in Milas, Hedef Vinç provides overhaul, fault diagnosis, repair and periodic maintenance for cranes of every make and model.

A slight hydraulic leak, a crushed section of wire rope or a delayed response from the controls is very often the first sign of a bigger failure on the way. Early diagnosis and planned maintenance always cost less than the losses caused by an unplanned breakdown.

Our Service Work Includes

  • General overhaul and detailed fault diagnosis for all crane brands
  • Hydraulic system maintenance: pump, valve, cylinder and hose checks
  • Inspection of wire ropes, hooks and pulleys, with replacements where needed
  • Boom and chassis weld repairs
  • Troubleshooting of electrical and control system faults
  • Periodic maintenance: oil, filter and grease service
  • Hydraulic hose pressing and hydraulic spare parts — at the same facility
  • In-house machining of pins, bushings and similar parts on our lathe

On-Site Service

If your crane cannot travel or cannot leave the job site, our service team comes to you anywhere around Milas, Bodrum, Muğla, Aydın and İzmir. We diagnose the fault in the field and complete whatever repairs are possible on the spot; jobs that call for a full overhaul are scheduled at our workshop, with the whole process planned together with you.

Why Hedef Vinç?

Because our hose-pressing machine, lathe and hydraulic spare parts stock are all under one roof, most repairs are turned around quickly without waiting for parts. We maintain our own crane fleet in the same workshop, so we know first-hand the failures that keep a crane standing idle on site. For crane service in Milas or crane repair in Bodrum, one phone call is all it takes.
