Operated Mobile Crane Hire in Milas & Bodrum

From our base in Milas, Hedef Vinç provides crane rental along the Bodrum, Muğla, Aydın and İzmir corridor. Every crane comes with an experienced operator — the lift plan, sling selection and on-site safety are our responsibility. With hourly, daily and project-based rental options, we match the crane to the scale of your job.

Our workshop sits between Güllük and Güvercinlik at the Tuzla junction, so we reach any point along the Milas–Bodrum road quickly. We are available by phone 24/7 for urgent work and plan night operations as well. English spoken: for enquiries in English, call our dedicated line directly at +90 532 294 48 63 — we regularly work with yacht owners, contractors and international residents around Bodrum.

What We Lift

  • Construction materials, steel structures and prefabricated elements
  • Air conditioners, pools, jacuzzis, glass panels and furniture — lifts to upper floors
  • Boat and yacht lifting, launching and haul-out operations
  • Containers, site equipment and generator placement
  • Lifting support for tree cutting and pruning work
  • Dismantling, moving and installing factory machinery
  • Totem, sign and billboard installation
  • Heavy equipment and vehicle recovery

Why Work with Hedef Vinç?

When the company that rents the crane is the same one that maintains it, site safety improves too. Our cranes are overhauled and serviced in our own workshop, hydraulics included, so the machine that arrives on your site is always well maintained and ready to work. We carry out a free site survey before the job and agree the price up front — no surprise costs.

How Is Pricing Determined?

Crane rental pricing depends on the weight of the load, the working height and radius, the duration of the job and the address. For most jobs, a short description over the phone is enough for us to give you a clear price straight away. For complex operations we send a quote after a free site survey.
