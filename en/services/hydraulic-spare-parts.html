Hydraulic Spare Parts Supply in Milas

A hydraulic failure means lost working days for your machine. At our workshop in Milas, the Hedef Vinç team knows crane and heavy equipment hydraulics inside out: we diagnose the faulty component on site or in the workshop and quickly source the correct replacement for your system. There is no need to waste time and money on the wrong part — we identify each component by its dimensions and operating values before handing it over.

We supply parts to construction sites, boatyards and businesses around Milas, Bodrum, Muğla, Aydın and İzmir. In most cases, sending us a photo of the part on WhatsApp is all it takes for us to find the right match.

Parts We Supply

  • Hydraulic pumps
  • Hydraulic valves and control blocks
  • Hydraulic cylinders (including piston rods and barrels)
  • Seal and gasket kits
  • Fittings and connectors
  • Hydraulic hoses
  • Hydraulic filters

Parts and Fitting from a Single Source

We do not simply hand over the part and leave you to find a mechanic. On request, we remove and install the pump, cylinder or seal kit we supply at our workshop — and for machines that cannot be moved, we provide on-site fitting. After installation we run the system and check pressure and leaks before handing the machine back. Both the part and the labour come from one place, with one point of contact.

If a Part Cannot Be Found, We Make It

For older machines, or systems no longer supported on the market, finding a spare part can be difficult. In such cases we take measurements from the original and manufacture the part in our machining workshop. For machinable components such as shafts, bushings, pins and piston rods, "unavailable" is never the final answer — we produce the part and, if needed, fit it in place.
