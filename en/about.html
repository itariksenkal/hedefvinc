Who Is Hedef Vinç?

Hedef Vinç is a company operating under the Retrofit Hedef Crane brand, providing both crane rental and crane & heavy equipment service at its facility between Güllük and Güvercinlik in Milas. Our site sits at the Tuzla junction, right at the midpoint of the Milas–Bodrum road, which lets us reach construction sites, marinas and businesses across the region in a short time.

Our team works with years of hands-on field experience. We do not simply rent out cranes; we are a crew that overhauls, maintains and services them ourselves, hydraulics included. Because we know every side of the job — from lift plans and sling selection to fault diagnosis and part manufacturing — we deliver fast, lasting solutions to the problems that come up on site.

Across Milas, Bodrum, Muğla, Aydın, İzmir and the surrounding area, we work with customers from every sector — construction, marine, agriculture and industry — with the same approach: giving you straight answers on the phone and delivering the job safely and on time.

What We Do

Everything Under One Roof

Under the same roof we provide crane rental, crane repair and maintenance, heavy equipment service, hydraulic hose pressing, hydraulic spare parts, machining and lathe works, welding, truck chassis works and container sales & rental — the complete range of services.

The benefit to you is simple: your job is never left waiting. When a machine bursts a hose in the field, the hose is pressed at our facility straight away; a part that cannot be sourced is made on our lathe; any welding needed is done the same day. Because the company that rents you the crane is the same one that maintains it, the machine that arrives on site is always well-kept and ready — and if anything does go wrong, the fix is not handed off to another service; our own team completes it on the spot.
