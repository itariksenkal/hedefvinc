The people who rent the crane also repair it

Hedef Vinç is run by Ahmet Uğur and Mehmet Uğur and has been working in Milas since 2005. Our workshop is between Güllük and Güvercinlik, at the Tuzla junction on the Milas–Bodrum road, behind the Shell station.

In the workshop we strip cranes down, rebuild their hydraulics and booms, repaint them and hand them back to their owners. Cranes that leave our workshop carry the Retrofit Hedef Crane sticker. Hose pressing, a lathe and welding are under the same roof, so most repairs do not wait for parts.

We service our own rental cranes in the same workshop, so we know the condition of every crane we send out.

For villa and boat owners around Bodrum

Typical jobs for villa and boat owners:

  • Lifting pool equipment, hot tubs and large planters into a garden
  • Putting air-conditioning units on a roof or terrace
  • Getting large glass panels, doors and furniture to upper floors
  • Placing a container or a site cabin on a plot
  • Lifting a boat out of the water, or back in at the start of the season
  • Repairing the crane on your boat or your truck

Bodrum's streets are narrow and steep. If we are not sure a crane will get in, we come and look first.

Frequently asked questions

Do you speak English?
Yes. Call our English line on +90 532 294 48 63. You can also send photos, videos and your location on WhatsApp.
Does the crane come with an operator?
Yes. Our cranes come with our own operator. Planning the lift and choosing the slings is our job; you only need to show us where the load should go.
How is the price worked out?
It depends on the weight and size of the load, how high and how far it has to go, how long the job takes, the address and the ground where the crane will stand. We give you a clear price once we have seen the job. We do not have a fixed price list.
Can you get a crane into a narrow street in Bodrum?
Often yes, but not always. The access road, tight corners, slopes and the space needed for the outriggers all matter. If we are not sure, we come and look first. A location pin and a few photos of the street usually tell us enough.
Do I have to bring a broken crane to your workshop?
Not always. We fix many hose, fitting, control and outrigger faults on site. Seal replacement, welding and full overhauls are done in our workshop at the Tuzla junction in Milas.
Which areas do you cover?
Milas, Bodrum, Muğla, Aydın, İzmir and the surrounding area, including the whole Bodrum peninsula. For other places, call and ask.
