A Burst Hose Means a Machine at a Standstill

Hydraulic hoses burst at the worst possible moment: while the crane is under load, the excavator is mid-dig or the truck is tipping its bed. From that moment the machine stops, and every passing hour means delays, an idle crew and a site falling behind schedule. That is exactly why our workshop in Milas handles hose pressing without a queue: bring in the old hose, we take its measurements, cut a new one, fit the couplings and crimp it on the press. Most jobs are ready the same day — many while you wait.

We keep hydraulic hose in a range of diameters and pressure ratings in stock, along with a wide selection of couplings. That means you get your machine running again quickly, without ever hearing "the part is on order, it will arrive next week".

Our Hose Pressing Service

  • Cut to size: We prepare the hose to suit its routing on your machine — neither too short nor left sagging.
  • Coupling assembly: We fit straight and elbow couplings in various thread types and crimp them on the press.
  • Matched to the pressure rating: We recommend the right hose class and number of plies for the circuit's working pressure.
  • Exact copy of the old hose: Just bring in the burst hose; we reproduce its length, diameter and coupling angles one to one.
  • Delivered from stock: Common diameters and coupling types are on the shelf, so most hoses are pressed the same day with no waiting on orders.

Which Machines Do We Press Hoses For?

From mobile crane and loader crane hoses to excavators, wheel loaders and backhoe loaders, and from tractors and agricultural machinery to tipper trucks and lorry hydraulic circuits — we press hoses for any machine that runs on hydraulics. Our workshop sits between Güllük and Güvercinlik at the Tuzla junction, a regular stop for mechanics and operators from around Milas, Bodrum, Muğla, Aydın and İzmir. If you cannot remove the hose yourself, give us a call and we will plan the most practical solution together.
