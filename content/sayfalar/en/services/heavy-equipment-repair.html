Heavy Equipment Service in Milas

A machine standing idle on site costs you money every single day. At our workshop in Milas, Hedef Vinç carries out mechanical and hydraulic repairs on heavy equipment and takes care of scheduled maintenance. From engine, transmission and undercarriage work to hydraulic pump, valve and cylinder overhauls, we diagnose the fault where the machine stands, explain the fix clearly and hand the job back on the agreed date.

Because hose pressing, lathe machining and welding are all available under one roof, most jobs are finished in a single stop: a burst hose is pressed on the spot, worn pins and bushings are machined on our lathe, and a cracked chassis or bucket is reinforced by welding. You do not lose days waiting for parts.

Machines We Service

  • Excavators (tracked and wheeled)
  • Loaders and mini loaders
  • Backhoe loaders
  • Forklifts and warehouse stacking equipment
  • Telescopic handlers (telehandlers)
  • Rollers, graders and other road machinery
  • Cranes and truck-mounted hydraulic equipment

What We Do

  • Engine servicing and repair, oil and filter changes
  • Transmission and travel (final drive) motor work
  • Undercarriage: tracks, rollers, sprockets, tensioning systems
  • Hydraulic pump, valve and cylinder repair; seal and gasket renewal
  • Hydraulic hose pressing and line replacement
  • Pin and bushing replacement, parts machined on the lathe
  • Welding repairs on buckets, booms and chassis
  • Scheduled maintenance: oils, filters, grease points, general inspection

On-Site Service

Not every machine can make it to the workshop — so we come to yours. Whether it is a construction site, marble quarry, farm or harbour anywhere along the Milas, Bodrum, Muğla, Aydın and İzmir corridor, our service vehicle reaches you and we deal with the breakdown on the spot. Describe the fault over the phone and we bring the necessary parts and equipment with us, finishing most jobs in a single visit. For emergencies you can reach us 24/7.
