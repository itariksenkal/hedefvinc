Hedef Vinç and Retrofit Hedef Crane

Hedef Vinç is a workshop that both repairs cranes and rents them out. Retrofit Hedef Crane is the name we put on the cranes we rebuild: retrofitting means taking an older machine apart, renewing it and making it ready for work again. You will see the name on our trucks and on cranes that have been through our workshop.

Because the people who hire out the crane are the same people who maintain it, we know the condition of every crane we send to a job. If something goes wrong, the fix is not handed to another workshop.

On Google you can also find us as Hedef Vinç Hidrolik; read or write a review on our Google profile.

Hose pressing, a lathe and welding are under the same roof. A burst hose is pressed in the workshop, a missing pin or bush is turned on the lathe, and the welding is done on the spot.

How we work

  1. Straight answers

    We listen to the job and tell you what it needs. If we do not know something, we say so.

  2. The price up front

    We price the job once we have seen it. No surprise items when the work is done.

  3. Safety first

    A lift plan and the right slings on every job. We do not take on unsafe lifts.

  4. On time

    We turn up at the time we agreed. Phones open day and night, emergencies included.
